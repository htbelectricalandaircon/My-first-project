Good lighting changes how a room looks and feels. Whether you want to swap old halogen downlights for energy-efficient LEDs, hang pendants over the kitchen bench, or add LED strip lighting to stairs, robes and cabinets, we'll help you choose the right fittings and install them neatly.

We take care to make minimal mess, patch around new fittings where needed, and leave your home clean and tidy.

Lighting we install

  • LED downlights and halogen-to-LED replacements
  • Pendant lights over benches, dining tables and entries
  • LED strip lighting for stairs, kitchens, robes and shelving
  • Chandeliers and feature lights, including high ceilings
  • Backlit LED mirrors and bathroom lighting
  • Outdoor, garden and security lighting
  • Dimmers, smart switches and two-way switching

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you replace my halogen downlights with LEDs?

Yes. LED downlights use a fraction of the energy, run much cooler and last far longer. We can usually reuse the existing holes or fit a new layout.

Do you supply the lights?

We can supply quality fittings or install lights you've already bought.

Can you install lights on a high or raked ceiling?

Yes, we install chandeliers and feature lights on high and raked ceilings.

Can I put my lights on a dimmer?

Most LED lights can be dimmed with a compatible dimmer. We'll check your fittings and recommend one that works with them.
