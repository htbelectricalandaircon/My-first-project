A Family Business You Can Trust

Welcome to HTB Electrical & Air Conditioning, a family-owned North Sydney business with more than 10 years of experience looking after homes across Sydney.

We treat every customer like family, and you always come first. Every job, big or small, gets the same care and attention we’d give our own family’s home. Whether you need a quick electrical repair, a new installation, or air conditioning to keep you comfortable all year round, our friendly team will turn up on time, explain things in plain English, and get the job done right the first time.

Our work is reliable, professional, and always completed to meet safety requirements and Australian Standards. And we have one golden rule: we leave your place clean and tidy, just like we were never there. No mess, no dust, no leftover offcuts, just great work you’ll be happy with.

As a family business, our name is on every job, and we take that seriously. Many of our customers have been with us for years, and we’d love to look after you too.

Give us a call today and see the difference a family business makes.
